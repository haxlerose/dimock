There is a great Pavilion on the grounds and a pond. We welcome churches and others to use our facilities. Talk to us about this.

Sun, Aug 9

Rick Marsi

A freelance writer, photographer, lecturer and nature guide, Rick graduated from Colgate University, is a former Peace Corps Volunteer in India and has served as director of Broome County's Finch Hollow Nature Center. Beginning in 1980, Rick wrote a syndicated nature column for Gannet Central New York Media every week for 44 years. He contributed hundreds of outdoor and environment stories, and as a photographer and lecturer, Rick has given hundreds of slide programs on various outdoor topics. His photographs have appeared in Sports Afield, Men's Health, Bird Watcher's Digest, Birder's World, and Reader's Digest Books. His work reflects a deep, spiritual appreciation for light and the natural world.

Sun, Aug 23

Lisa Whitaker

Originally from Absecon, NJ, Lisa grew up coming to Dimock every summer, a tradition rooted in her family's 25-year ownership of a cottage there, spanning from the 1970s to the early 1990's. With 35 years of service in Youth With A Mission (YWAM), Lisa has traveled to 88 countries and continues to explore. She has a deep passion for experiencing new cultures and connecting with people around the globe. She holds a Master's in Christian Formation and Discipleship and works with the International Discipleship Training School Centre (IDTSC), which supports and trains YWAM's DTS globally. Her ministry involves leading discipleship schools, teaching, and conducting training seminars and retreats. Passionate about sharing stories of God's faithfulness worldwide, she is eager to present the new opportunities He has placed before her.

Sun, Aug 30

Patti Yoder

Patti Yoder's presentation will center on her 2012 strokes and the near-death experience that birthed Wisdom From Beyond, serving as the catalyst for a profound life shift. By bridging these spiritual insights with the physical and emotional realities of her 14-year recovery, Patti illustrates how to move past the label of "patient" to truly embody the life of a "living miracle." This integration turns lessons from the "other side" into a practical toolkit for modern living, offering a reflective roadmap for finding purpose and shared wisdom through long-term healing.
